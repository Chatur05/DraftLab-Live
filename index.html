Method, strategy basis, and known data conflicts

Self-contained board for 12-team, 18-round Underdog Best Ball Mania VII (half PPR, QB/RB/RB/WR/WR/WR/TE/FLEX). Player pool and ADP snapshot dated July 24, 2026. The engine encodes the strategy in 12ManDraftGuide.md: a dynamic 2–3 QB / 4–5 RB / 6–9 WR / 2–3 TE shell centred on 3/5/7/3; RB and WR timing checkpoints; QB patience with an exact two-QB finish only after the completed room passes quality, security, bye, and stack gates; either a premium TE room or a later three-TE room; attainable direct QB stacks; and quality-based Week 16/17 correlation. Raw playoff-player count is context only, never a quota.

Judgment layers (my estimates, not measured data)

  • Current-news overlay is a separate, expiring layer for breaking injury and role reports. Active records may temporarily lower availability, add a recommendation penalty, or require a market discount. Every record has sources and an expiration date; expired news has zero impact.
  • Availability weights drive the live-player projection. They are coarse priors informed by the guide's flagged situations (Rice suspension risk, Nabers participation, Kittle's January Achilles, Aiyuk's unsettled landing spot) plus free-agent status. They are not injury projections.
  • Team environment tiers boost stack value for offenses the guide flags as favorable Week 17 environments (Cowboys, Bengals, Lions, Bears, Rams) plus generally strong offenses. Directional only.
  • QB style (pocket / hybrid / rushing) sizes stack bonuses, following the guide's point that rushing QBs score uncorrelated to their pass catchers and therefore need less stacking.

Conflicts between the guide and the schedule/bye data

  • The guide references a Week 17 CIN/ARI game for a McBride bring-back. The schedule map has CIN–BAL and ARI–LV. The board uses the schedule map.
  • The guide says six teams are on a Week 14 bye, including the Ravens and Texans. The bye map has only ARI and DAL on Week 14 (BAL is 13, HOU is 8). The board uses the bye map — and because Week 14 is the final regular-season week, it warns separately about stacking ARI/DAL.
  • The guide's "Week 11 Byemageddon (Eagles, Bears, Rams, Packers)" is partly off: PHI and CHI are on Week 10. Week 11 does carry six teams (CLE, ATL, GB, NE, LAR, SEA). Bye clusters are computed from the data, not hard-coded.

Caveats carried over from the guide

  • The BBM VI advance-rate figures are descriptive of the 2025 season, read off graphs, and approximate. The field adapts; replicating last year's optimal build exactly is itself a risk. RB-early is weighted as a strong preference, not a rule.
  • 2-QB vs 3-QB is close and year-dependent. The board defaults to 3 and switches to an exact 2 only after the complete two-QB room passes market-quality, starter-security, health/leash, bye, and direct-stack qualification. It never targets 1 or 4+.
  • ADP moves daily. Re-check before drafting, especially rookies and the flagged injury/suspension situations.

This is a decision aid, not a guarantee.
